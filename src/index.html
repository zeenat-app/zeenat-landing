COMMON QUESTIONS

About the app

What can I do with Zeenat?

Search businesses by category and location, including fashion, beauty, weddings and events. View portfolios, send service requests, save measurements, buy and sell marketplace items, and share community posts.

Can I showcase my own business?

Yes. Zeenat includes business profiles, portfolios, service listings and tools for managing customer service requests.

Are the businesses on this website real listings?

The previews on this page use illustrative demo content. Live public business and marketplace pages are planned for a future update.
